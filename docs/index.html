02 / FIVE WHYS

Power is useful.
Clarity is essential.

Your terminal is already where the work happens. Bodek gives the agent a place in that workflow.

Explore every feature ↗
  1. 01

    WHY STAY IN THE TERMINAL?

    Keep your hands
    where the work happens.

    Navigate turns, switch models, queue prompts, and return to a session with the keyboard. Launch a local engine or attach to one you already run.

  2. 02

    WHY A CALM INTERFACE?

    Attention is part
    of the budget.

    Compact tool rows and turn receipts make progress easy to scan. Details expand on demand, and scrolling back to read doesn’t surrender your place to incoming output.

  3. 03

    WHY STRUCTURED RESULTS?

    Confidence
    needs receipts.

    Inspect diffs, test verdicts, and per-tool durations. Sub-agent chips show who is working and who failed. Missing success metadata stays neutral; prose alone never earns a green check.

  4. 04

    WHY A SEPARATE FRONT-END?

    A richer terminal.
    A focused engine.

    Bodek brings the Bubble Tea interface. Odek owns tools, memory, sandboxing, and execution policy. Presentation dependencies stay in the client; security decisions stay with the engine.

    See the integration contract ↗
  5. 05

    WHY OPEN SOURCE?

    Read both sides
    of the connection.

    Trace how Bodek presents an action and how Odek enforces its boundaries. Both projects are MIT licensed, with source you can inspect, modify, and build yourself.

    Inspect Bodek ↗ Inspect Odek ↗
